## Brand & Style

This design system serves modern non-profit development directors, donor relations officers, and field fundraisers who demand the speed and tactile precision of a mobile-first operations tool. The emotional tone balances absolute financial trustworthiness with human warmth and civic momentum. 

The aesthetic is Modern Corporate with Tactile Clarity: precision hairline borders, disciplined data density, crisp card surfaces, and rich semantic cues. Whitespace is intentional rather than decorative, allowing relationship managers to scan donation histories, pledge statuses, and donor lifecycles under direct sunlight or in fast-paced donor meetings. Visual weight communicates operational competence, stewardship, and urgency without visual noise.

## Colors

The palette establishes institutional trust anchored by deep navy, balanced by vibrant positive validation tones.

- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: The commanding slate-navy anchor. Applied to active navigation, primary calls-to-action, major headers, and vital metrics. It communicates security, institutional stability, and structural clarity.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`)**: A vibrant emerald green symbolizing gift fulfillment, positive financial flow, goal completions, and recurring pledge renewals.
- **Tertiary (`#6366F1`)**: A modern royal indigo deployed for forward-looking signals—such as planned giving, campaign milestones, relationship tags, and analytical projections.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Balanced cool slate used for field outlines, secondary metadata, inactive iconography, and contextual hints.
- **Background & Canvas**: High-efficiency contrast utilizing `#F8FAFC` as the application foundation, `#FFFFFF` for elevated data cards, and `#F1F5F9` for secondary structural dividers, sub-panels, and disabled states.
- **Semantic Status Signals**:
  - `Success / Completed`: `#059669` text on `#ECFDF5` fill.
  - `Recurring Active`: `#0284C7` text on `#F0F9FF` fill.
  - `Major Donor / VIP`: `#7C3AED` text on `#F5F3FF` fill.
  - `Pledged / Pending`: `#D97706` text on `#FFFBEB` fill.
  - `Lapsed / Alert`: `#DC2626` text on `#FEF2F2` fill.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines, campaign milestones, and currency metrics with **Inter** for transactional records, contact dossiers, and metadata listings.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** injects structured optimism, clean geometric proportions, and clear tabular-lining numbers for rapid reading of gift amounts, pledge goals, and summary totals.
- **Inter** provides utilitarian clarity for high-density tables, multi-line contact interaction notes, task lists, and compact filter controls.
- Numeric metrics must consistently enable tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during filtering, tallying, or live gift stream refreshes.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system utilizes an 8-point structural grid with a 4-point micro-grid for fine adjustments (badges, icon alignments, and compact list counters).

- **Mobile Viewport (< 640px)**: A 4-column fluid layout with `16px` (`1rem`) side margins and `12px` (`0.75rem`) internal gutters. Tap targets maintain a strict 44×44px interactive minimum despite dense data presentation. Vertical rhythm favors compact modules (`12px` to `16px` separation) to display complete donor interactions above the standard mobile fold.
- **Tablet / Split View (640px – 1024px)**: An 8-column layout with `24px` margins. Optimizes for a two-pane master-detail pattern (donor directory left, full ledger/dossier right).
- **Desktop / Workstation (> 1024px)**: A 12-column layout capped at `1280px` maximum viewport width, preserving dense administrative overview layouts without overextending horizontal scan paths.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids excessive shadows, relying instead on structural layering, tactile border highlights, and low-offset ambient groundings:

- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas)**: `#F8FAFC` flat surface.
- **Surface Level 1 (Default Cards & Dossiers)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed with a crisp `1px` solid border (`#E2E8F0`). Shadow is intentionally subtle: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active State)**: Applied to pressed cards, active bottom sheets, and elevated filter chips. Bound with `1px` `#CBD5E1` and lifted by `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Action Modals & Navigation Sheets)**: `#FFFFFF` encased in `1px` `#94A3B8` (15% opacity), anchored with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`. Accompanied by a 30% alpha backdrop overlay of `#0F172A` with `4px` blur.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`) to humanize the interface while preserving structural density:

- **Standard Containers & Cards**: `12px` (`0.75rem`) to `16px` (`1rem`) corner radii, framing donor details crisply without appearing boxy or juvenile.
- **Input Fields & Form Selectors**: `8px` (`0.5rem`) for a defined, tactile data-entry contour.
- **Badges, Tags, and Filter Chips**: Complete pills (`9999px`) to immediately differentiate categorical statuses, recurring flags, and dynamic donor tiers from clickable functional panels.
- **Micro-Indicators (Avatars & Metric Nodes)**: `8px` for system initials or fully circular (`50%`) for authenticated donor profile pictures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #0F172A`. On press: scale down 0.98, background `#1E293B`. Height 44px (mobile standard), corner radius `8px`.
- **Secondary / Operational**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. On press: background `#F8FAFC`.
- **Success / Donation Entry**: Background `#10B981`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used strictly for "Log Gift", "Approve Pledge", and "Confirm Donation". Hover/active `#059669`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Filter Chips**: Height 32px, radius `9999px`. 
  - Unselected: background `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid transparent`, text `#475569`.
  - Selected: background `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`.
  - Count counter attached inside chip: 16px circular badge with inverted contrast.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- Rendered with `badge` typography in uppercase, medium weight with matching light tint backgrounds:
  - **Recurring**: `#0284C7` on `#F0F9FF`, border `1px solid #BAE6FD`.
  - **One-time**: `#475569` on `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
  - **Major Donor**: `#7C3AED` on `#F5F3FF`, border `1px solid #DDD6FE`.
  - **Pledged**: `#D97706` on `#FFFBEB`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
  - **Received**: `#059669` on `#ECFDF5`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.

### Cards & Donor Dossiers
- Background `#FFFFFF`, radius `12px`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Internal padding: `12px 16px`. Features a top-line donor name in `headline-sm`, trailing donation total in `headline-sm` with tabular lining numbers, secondary subline displaying gift frequency and latest interaction timestamp in `body-sm` (`#64748B`), and a bottom row of status badges.

### Lists & Feed Items
- Borderless rows separated by `1px solid #F1F5F9` dividers. Row height 56px minimum. Left side holds donor initials avatar (36×36px, `#F1F5F9` fill, `#0F172A` text), center holds donor identity and campaign label, right side features right-aligned currency amount in `body-md` bold above transaction date.

### Input Fields
- Height 44px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `8px`, padding `0 12px`.
- Active focus state: border `1px solid #0F172A`, ring `2px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- Currency Input Variant: Permanent prefixed currency symbol `$`, locked left in `#64748B`, with primary value typed in `headline-md` weight.

### Quick-Log Donation Sheet
- Bottom-docked sheet with swipe handles, direct numerical keypads, campaign assignment split-tabs, and segmented payment type selectors (Credit, ACH, Check, Stock, In-Kind).
