END OF SERVICE BENEFIT (EOSB) CALCULATION GUIDE

Bandama Charity Foundation Sierra Leone • Human Resources & Finance

Purpose & Scope

To provide a transparent and fair method for computing employees’ end-of-service entitlement in accordance with organizational policy and Sierra Leone labor practice.

1. What is End of Service Benefit?

The End of Service Benefit (EOSB) is a lump-sum payment made to an employee at the end of their employment, recognizing their years of service, loyalty, and contribution to the Foundation. It applies when an employee retires, resigns properly, or completes a contract — but not when dismissed for gross misconduct.

2. Eligibility Criteria

An employee qualifies for EOSB if they:

  • Have served at least 1 year continuously;
  • Are leaving due to resignation, retirement, contract completion, redundancy, or medical grounds;
  • Have no pending disciplinary cases or debts with the Foundation.
3. Core Formula for Calculation
EOSB = Basic Monthly Salary × Years of Service × Benefit Rate
4. Recommended Benefit Rate Table
Years of Service Benefit Rate Description
1 – 2 years 25% of 1 month salary per year Probationary / short service
3 – 5 years 50% of 1 month salary per year Moderate service
6 – 10 years 75% of 1 month salary per year Long service
> 10 years 100% of 1 month salary per year Extended senior service
5. Example Calculation (from Policy)

Let’s say an employee served 6 years with a basic monthly salary of 10,000 SLE:

EOSB = 10,000 × 6 × 0.75 = 45,000 SLE

So, the employee will receive 45,000 SLE as their end-of-service benefit (plus any optional additions).

6. Optional Additions (if policy allows)
Component Description Example
Leave Balance Compensation Unused leave days paid in cash (No. of days × Daily Rate)
Gratuity Bonus 5–10% of total EOSB for excellent performance e.g., 45,000 × 0.05 = 2,250 SLE
Severance Adjustment For contract completion (if donor-funded) As per donor agreement
7. Excel Formula Implementation
Years of Service: =DATEDIF(Start_Date, End_Date, "Y")
Benefit Rate: =IF(C2<=2, 0.25, IF(C2<=5, 0.5, IF(C2<=10, 0.75, 1)))
EOSB: =Basic_Salary * Years_of_Service * Benefit_Rate
8. Approval and Payment Process
  1. HR verifies employee records and years of service.
  2. Finance computes EOSB using approved formula.
  3. Internal Audit reviews for compliance.
  4. CEO and Governing Board approve payment.
  5. Payment made via payroll system or direct transfer.
  6. Employee signs End of Service Acknowledgment Form.
9. Record Keeping

All EOSB records are maintained by HR and Finance for at least 5 years, both in hard and soft copies.
