groups Director-Level Departmental Access Specifications

Finance Director

Access: Budgets, expenditure, donor funds, financial transactions, project financial reports, payroll information where authorized.
Restriction: Cannot modify HR employee records.

HRM Director

Access: Employee records, recruitment, accounts provisioning, attendance, leave, contracts, payroll information, training, HR reports.
Authority: Full HR operational authority.

Programs Director

Access: All project records, work plans, beneficiary registries, project indicators, field activities, project reports.
Restriction: View-only financial data.

Internal Risk & Audits

Access: Audit dashboard, risk register, compliance records, audit trails, financial control reports, procurement controls.
Restriction: Read/audit access; cannot alter records audited.

IT Director

Access: User accounts, technical system admin, configuration, technical logs, security monitoring, backup management.
Restriction: No access to confidential HR/beneficiary data.

Health & Safety Director

Access: Health & safety records, incident reports, safety inspections, risk assessments, staff safety training records.

Legal & Communication

Access: Legal records, contracts, agreements, compliance documentation, litigation records, organizational communications.

Procurement Director

Access: Procurement plans, suppliers, purchase requests, quotations, evaluations, purchase orders, goods received notes.
