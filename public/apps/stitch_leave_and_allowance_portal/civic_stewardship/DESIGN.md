## Brand & Style

This design system serves an institutional NGO human resources and fiscal management portal operating in Sierra Leone. The brand embodies institutional stewardship, civic transparency, and compassionate professionalism. The interface must communicate unquestionable accountability, rigorous governance, and administrative clarity to humanitarian field workers, country directors, and financial auditors alike.

The aesthetic blends **Corporate / Modern** governance with tactile clarity:
- **Visual Weight**: Uncluttered, structured, and legible under varying light and network conditions (e.g., field offices in Bo, Kenema, Makeni, and Freetown).
- **Tone**: Authoritative yet accessible, reliable, unpretentious, and reassuring.
- **Stylistic Signifiers**: Subtle nods to national pride via deep botanical emerald, balanced maritime navy, crisp alabaster surfaces, and discreet golden-amber operational accents.
- **Interaction Ethos**: Low friction, explicit confirmation states, unambiguous audit trails, and instant status comprehension.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in Sierra Leone’s ecological and maritime heritage, reimagined for institutional durability:

- **Primary (`#0D5C3A` - Deep Emerald)**: Represents institutional integrity, vitality, and organizational grounding. Used for top-level navigation headers, primary submission actions, authorized approval badges, and primary key metrics.
- **Secondary (`#1B365D` - Atlantic Slate Navy)**: Conveys fiscal governance, administrative rigor, and stability. Utilized for secondary action tiers, data tables, structural headers, and financial audit metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#C48208` - Ochre Gold)**: Reflects civic stewardship and pending status alerts. Dedicated to actionable alerts, warning indicators, and pending verification stages.
- **Neutral (`#505A5E` - Basalt Slate)**: A balanced, low-chroma slate neutral avoiding muddy gray tones, providing high contrast for body typography, dividers, and structural outlines.

### Functional Status Tokens
- **Status Eligible**: Emerald Tint Background (`#EAF5EF`), Foreground (`#0B4E31`), Border (`#BCE3CD`).
- **Status Pending**: Amber Tint Background (`#FEF7E6`), Foreground (`#8C5D05`), Border (`#F8DE9E`).
- **Status Approved / Processed**: Navy Tint Background (`#EFF4FA`), Foreground (`#122744`), Border (`#BED3EC`).
- **Status Rejected / Alert**: Crimson Background (`#FDECEE`), Foreground (`#941927`), Border (`#F7B6BD`).

## Typography

**Public Sans** is utilized universally across all typographic roles. Engineered for institutional administration and high-legibility government portals, it delivers clarity on degraded screens, handles dense tabular data with precision, and provides uniform vertical metrics.

### Typographic Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Numbers and Currency (SLL / NLe)**: Use the `numeric-data` tier configured with tabular lining numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to align allowance sums and leave day balances consistently across ledger views.
- **Field Labels and Metadata**: Strictly deploy `label-md` or `label-sm` in uppercase or heavy tracking to establish an explicit structural relationship with user inputs and status summaries.
- **Paragraph Flow**: Keep line length for policy disclosures and leave remarks restricted to a 60–75 character measure to preserve reading comfort on tablets and desktop monitors.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture leverages an 8-point base rhythm, stepping down to a 4-point sub-grid for compact, data-dense tabular interfaces and summary cards.

### Grid System
- **Desktop (≥1024px)**: 12-column fluid grid, max container width of `1280px`, `2rem` gutters, and `2rem` margins. The operational layout employs a persistent 260px administrative sidebar paired with a content workspace.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout, `1.5rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` margins. The navigation converts to an off-canvas drawer or top persistent summary bar.
- **Mobile (≤767px)**: 4-column layout, `1rem` margins, and `0.75rem` gutters. Form fields switch strictly to single-column vertical stacks. Tabular balance summaries collapse into self-contained stacked audit cards.

### Dense Data Accommodations
In allowance calculation screens and leave ledger tables, internal cell padding compresses to `space-sm` (vertical) and `space-md` (horizontal) to permit cross-department comparisons without excessive horizontal scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system eschews floating, highly blurred shadows in favor of **structural containment, low-contrast borders, and subtle tonal layering**. This reinforces institutional permanence and preserves visual clarity on low-brightness screens in remote locations.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Background color `#F8FAF9`. Solid, clean, reducing glare.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a crisp `1px` low-contrast border in `#DFE5E2`. No shadow required on desktop viewports.
3. **Level 2 (Active/Hover/Floating Modals)**: White surface with an ambient, deep-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.04)`. Outlined by a `1px` border in `#CFD8D3`.
4. **Level 3 (System Dialogs & Drawers)**: High-priority approval dialogs with an ambient spread: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(13, 92, 58, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.06)`, framed with `#BCE3CD`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** geometry. 

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Chips**: Set with a strict `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. This conveys architectural precision, civic utility, and functional directness.
- **Cards, Ledger Panels, and Modals**: Set with `rounded-lg` at `0.5rem` (8px), preventing elements from appearing severe while maintaining rigid grid alignment.
- **Avatar & Progress Metrics**: Circle geometries are restricted strictly to user identity anchors and circular progress indicators. Form fields and status indicators remain rectilinear with softened corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action/Submit/Approve)**: Solid `#0D5C3A` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` border-radius, `12px 20px` padding (`label-lg`). Hover: `#09452B`. Focus ring: `2px` solid `#C48208` with `2px` offset.
- **Secondary (HR Review/Save Draft)**: Solid `#1B365D` fill, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover: `#132744`.
- **Tertiary / Outline (Cancel/Return)**: Pure white background, `1px` border in `#505A5E`, text `#1B365D`. Hover: `#F0F4F2`.
- **Destructive (Reject Application)**: Background `#FFFFFF`, `1px` border in `#D32F2F`, text `#D32F2F`. Hover: `#FDECEE`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Displayed with `label-sm` in tabular uppercase formatting, `4px 8px` padding, and `4px` border-radius:
  - **Eligible**: Background `#EAF5EF`, text `#0B4E31`, border `1px solid #BCE3CD`.
  - **Pending**: Background `#FEF7E6`, text `#8C5D05`, border `1px solid #F8DE9E`.
  - **Approved**: Background `#EFF4FA`, text `#122744`, border `1px solid #BED3EC`.
  - **Processed / Disbursed**: Background `#0D5C3A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #0D5C3A`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, height `40px` (or `44px` on mobile for touch compliance), border `1px solid #CFD8D3`, text `#1B365D` using `body-md`.
- Active/Focused: Border `2px solid #0D5C3A`, no drop-shadow.
- Supporting label fixed above input in `label-md` with neutral `#505A5E`.
- Date range pickers (for annual/maternity leave windows) must display selected days count in an inline indicator badge (`numeric-data`).

### Cards & Ledger Tables
- **Summary Balance Cards**: White canvas, `1px solid #DFE5E2`, `16px` padding. Contains leave category title (`label-md`), big numerical balance (`display-lg`), and an embedded subtle allowance status tag.
- **Ledger Tables**: Explicit `1px solid #DFE5E2` horizontal rules. Table header with `#F4F7F5` background, `#1B365D` uppercase bold text (`label-sm`). Alternating row striping is disallowed; hover states highlight the full row using `#F8FAF9`.

### Approval Timeline & Signature Blocks
- A vertical stepper with `2px` connecting rules in `#DFE5E2`.
- Completed steps show a solid `#0D5C3A` disk with a checkmark; active pending steps show `#C48208` with an inner dot; upcoming steps show an empty ring in `#CFD8D3`.
- Designated digital sign-off areas include timestamp, unique verification hash, and officer designation in `body-sm`.
